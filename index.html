The Vision of Yechezkel, in white stone and gold

An explorable 3D Beit HaMikdash. The floor plan is the prophetic one of Yechezkel 40–48 — a 500 × 500 amah court, three gates and no gate in the west, the eastern gate sealed — raised at the scale of one world unit to one amah and finished in white stone, sea-wave marble and gold.

Thirty-six hidden things wait in the courts: eighteen silver rimonim and eighteen wonders, each one a teaching drawn from Tanach, Talmud, or the spade of the archaeologist. Among them:

  • Walk the courts in first person, or turn the House from above.
  • Speak with the kohanim at their stations of the morning avodah.
  • Strike the fifteen steps of the Levites and hear the Shir HaMa'alot, one degree of the ascent to a step.
  • Watch the fire on the altar from day into night, and the river run out from beneath the threshold.
